About Rickey

A real person before he is a “type.”

Rickey J. Green is a Louisville-based entrepreneur, recording artist, creative director, recovery advocate, husband, father, and public-facing community leader. Performing as Dolla Green, he has served as the principal on-camera presence in independent rap music videos and social productions since 2008.

His background behind the camera—directing, lighting, filming, planning, storyboarding, editing, light VFX, and audio work—makes him unusually aware of framing, continuity, timing, coverage, crew needs, and the difference between a strong moment and a usable take.
