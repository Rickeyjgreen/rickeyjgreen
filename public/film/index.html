About Rickey

Comfortable on both sides of the camera.

Rickey J. Green is a Louisville-based unit still photographer, cinematographer, camera operator, and director with three motion-picture credits and extensive visual-production experience dating to 2008.

His background includes directing, lighting, filming, planning, storyboarding, editing, light VFX, and audio-post work. As recording artist and performer Dolla Green, he has also served as principal on-camera talent in numerous productions—bringing a practical understanding of performance, blocking, visual storytelling, and the needs of talent.
